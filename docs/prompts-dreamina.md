# Prompts do Dreamina — filme da abertura

Formato: vertical 9:16, 1080p, 5 s cada. O clipe 2 parte do último quadro do 1, e o 3 do último quadro do 2.

## Imagem-base (texto para imagem)

Vertical 9:16 macro food photography. A single perfect scoop of purple grape ice cream in a crisp golden waffle cone, centered, on a dark deep-purple studio background. Tiny condensation droplets, creamy texture, soft lilac and magenta rim light, shallow depth of field, commercial ice cream advertising, ultra detailed. No text, no logo.

## Clipe 1 — sorvete girando (imagem para vídeo)

The purple ice cream cone slowly rotates in place while the camera makes a smooth slow orbit. Condensation glistens, soft lilac rim light, dark purple background. Slow motion, cinematic commercial food shot. No text.

## Clipe 2 — calda (a partir do último quadro do clipe 1)

Glossy dark chocolate syrup pours from above onto the purple ice cream scoop and slowly drips down its sides and over the waffle cone. Same dark purple background and lighting. Slow motion macro, cinematic. No text.

## Clipe 3 — derretimento (a partir do último quadro do clipe 2)

The camera slowly pulls back revealing the whole cone while the ice cream gently melts, creamy drips running down the cone. Leave empty dark purple space in the upper third of the frame. Same lighting, slow motion, cinematic. No text.
